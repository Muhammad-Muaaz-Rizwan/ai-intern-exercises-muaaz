Query: Explain what embeddings are.

Tool used: none

Answer:
Embeddings are numerical representations of data (such as words, sentences, or images) mapped into a vector space. In this space, items with similar meanings or contexts are positioned close together, allowing computers to understand semantic relationships and process language or data mathematically.

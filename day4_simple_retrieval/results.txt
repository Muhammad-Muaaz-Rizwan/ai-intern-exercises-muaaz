Query: How does a model learn from labeled data?

Retrieved Document: Doc1.txt

Query: What causes a model to perform poorly on new data?

Retrieved Document: Doc4.txt
Overfitting happens when a machine learning model learns the training data too well, including its noise, and performs poorly on new data.

Query: How are model parameters optimized?

Retrieved Document: Doc5.txt
